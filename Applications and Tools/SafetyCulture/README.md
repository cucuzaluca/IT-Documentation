## SafetyCulture

SafetyCulture is a workplace operations and safety management platform used to create and complete inspections, checklists, audits and other workplace processes. It allows organisations to digitally record inspection results, identify issues, assign corrective actions and maintain records of completed inspections.


## How its used
In a business environment, SafetyCulture can be used for workplace inspections, safety checks, equipment or site audits, reporting issues, assigning actions and maintaining compliance records.
