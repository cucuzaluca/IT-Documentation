## NAS
Stands for Network Attached Storage. A NAS is a storage device connected to a network that allows multiple computers and users to access
files from a central location.

Instead of having a hard drive physically plugged into one computer, you can have a NAS.The NAS normally has its own IP address and connects to your network, commonly through Ethernet.


### NAS vs normal external drive

The big difference is where the storage is attached:
- External HDD: attached directly to one computer.
- NAS: attached to the network and designed to provide storage to network users/devices.

A NAS may also contain multiple HDDs or SSDs and can use technologies such as RAID to provide additional capacity or redundancy.
