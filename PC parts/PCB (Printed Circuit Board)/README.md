### Solder
The green layer you see is called the solder mask. It's a thin protective coating placed over the copper underneath. It helps protect the 
copper from things like oxidation and prevents accidental electrical connections during soldering.

Underneath that is usually a rigid material such as fibreglass/epoxy (FR-4) that gives the board its physical structure.

### Copper Traces

A trace is basically a tiny flat electrical wire printed onto the PCB. They can carry electrical signals/data, but traces can also carry power and 
ground. And PCBs can have traces INSIDE them
